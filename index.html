Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to common questions about using GetCalcu calculators and privacy policies.

Do I need an account to use GetCalcu?

No. All calculators and utilities on GetCalcu are 100% free and immediately accessible without signing up, entering an email address, or creating an account.

Are my calculations and inputs private?

Yes. All calculations are executed locally inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Your financial figures, income data, and personal health measurements are never transmitted to our servers or stored in any database.

How do you ensure calculation accuracy?

Every calculator is implemented using standard mathematical formulas and published benchmarks from authoritative institutions (such as standard amortization formulas and government agency benchmarks). We maintain a suite of over 320 automated unit tests that continuously verify calculation outputs against reference standards.

Can I save, share, or print my results?

Yes. You can copy the browser URL with your inputs to share or bookmark your calculation, or use your browser's Print function (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) to create a clean, formatted PDF summary.

Are there any hidden fees or paid calculators?

None. Every calculator, converter, and utility across all 7 categories on GetCalcu is completely free to use without restrictions.
