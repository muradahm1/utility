How to Estimate Paint Coverage for Rooms

A gallon of quality interior paint covers approximately 350 to 400 square feet per coat.

Accounting for Openings

Standard doors subtract approximately 21 sq ft, while average windows subtract about 15 sq ft.

How to Use the Paint Calculator

  1. Step 1: Enter room length, width, and ceiling height.
  2. Step 2: Add number of doors and windows.
  3. Step 3: Choose 1 or 2 coats.
  4. Step 4: View required paint in gallons and liters.
Formula: Gallons = Net Area × Coats / 350
