How the US Navy Body Fat Formula Works & How to Set Goals

The US Navy body fat calculation is one of the most accurate circumference-based body composition methods, validated by clinical exercise physiology studies.

Why Lean Mass is the Key to Metabolism

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is determined almost entirely by your lean body mass (muscle, bone, organs). Preserving lean muscle with sufficient dietary protein (0.8-1.0g per lb of body weight) ensures your metabolism stays high while losing body fat.

Setting a Safe, Sustainable Calorie Deficit

A moderate 500-calorie daily deficit produces exactly 1.0 lb of pure fat loss per week without triggering muscle loss, hormonal fatigue, or metabolic adaptation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the US Navy formula compared to DEXA?

When measured accurately, the US Navy formula typically correlates within 3% to 4% of clinical DEXA scans and hydrostatic underwater weighing.

What is a realistic body fat percentage for natural athletes?

For men, 10% to 15% is athletic and sustainable year-round. For women, 18% to 24% provides a lean, defined physique while supporting healthy endocrine and reproductive function.

Where should I measure my waist circumference?

Men should measure horizontally across the navel (belly button). Women should measure at the narrowest natural waistline between the ribs and hips.

How often should I recalculate my body fat percentage?

Measure once every 2 to 4 weeks under identical conditions (morning, fasted) to track true fat loss and prevent daily water weight fluctuations from misleading you.
