How to Calculate Your Net Paycheck and Understand Tax Withholding

Your gross salary represents total earnings before federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security, Medicare, and voluntary pre-tax deductions (like 401k and healthcare).

FICA Taxes Explained

FICA consists of Social Security (6.2% on earnings up to the annual wage cap) and Medicare (1.45% on all earnings, plus an additional 0.9% for high earners).

Pre-Tax Deductions Save You Money

Contributing to traditional 401(k) accounts, HSAs, and pre-tax health insurance reduces your taxable income, lowering the amount of federal and state taxes withheld from each paycheck.

How to Use the Salary & Paycheck Calculator

Formula: Net Pay = Gross Income − Federal Tax − FICA Tax − State Tax − Pre-tax Deductions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between gross pay and net pay?

Gross pay is the total amount earned before any deductions or taxes. Net pay (take-home pay) is the remaining money deposited into your bank account after all taxes and deductions.

How does filing status affect paycheck withholding?

Filing status determines your standard deduction and income tax brackets. Married couples filing jointly have wider tax brackets, typically lowering tax withholding compared to single filers.

How much are FICA taxes?

FICA taxes equal 7.65% for employees (6.2% Social Security + 1.45% Medicare). Employers match an identical 7.65%.
