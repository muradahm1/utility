How to Calculate Concrete Volume Accurately

Ordering concrete accurately prevents expensive short-load fees or messy excess waste.

Cubic Yards Calculation

Concrete is measured in cubic yards (27 cubic feet). Multiply length by width by depth in feet, then divide by 27.

How to Use the Concrete Calculator

  1. Step 1: Select slab, footing, or column shape.
  2. Step 2: Enter dimensions in feet and thickness in inches.
  3. Step 3: Review cubic yards and bag count required.
Formula: yd³ = (ft × ft × (in / 12)) / 27
