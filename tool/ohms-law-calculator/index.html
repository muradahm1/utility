Understanding Ohm's Law in Electrical Engineering

Ohm's Law is the foundational principle of electrical circuits, defining the direct relationship between voltage, current, resistance, and electrical power.

The Core Relationships

Voltage (V) = Current (I) × Resistance (R). Power (P) = Voltage (V) × Current (I).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Ohm's Law?

Ohm's Law states that the current through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the voltage across the two points and inversely proportional to the resistance.
