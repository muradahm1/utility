How to Calculate Your Required Final Exam Grade

Knowing what you need on your final exam allows you to prioritize study time strategically across your courses.

Weighted Grading Formula

Final Exam Required = (Target Grade - (Current Grade × (1 - Final Weight))) ÷ Final Weight.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I need over 100% on the final?

If the required score exceeds 100%, achieving that grade is mathematically impossible without extra credit points or a grading curve from your instructor.
