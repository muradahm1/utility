The Student Success & Grade Mastery Guide

Clear, practical tools to calculate GPAs, predict final exam requirements, and plan study loads.

Strategy 1

How Credit-Weighted GPA Actually Works

College and high school GPAs are weighted by credit hours: earning an A in a 4-credit science lecture carries twice the mathematical weight of an A in a 2-credit lab course. Protect your GPA by prioritizing study time on high-credit classes.

Strategy 2

The Final Exam Math: Know Exactly What Score You Need

Before stressing about finals week, calculate the exact minimum score required on the final exam to maintain your desired course grade. Often, a student only needs a 72% or 78% on the final to lock in their "B" or "A".

Plain-English Terms You Need to Know

Credit Hours
The number of classroom hours per week assigned to a course, determining how heavily it influences your GPA.
Unweighted GPA
Standard 4.0 scale where every class is graded on the same numerical scale regardless of difficulty.
Weighted GPA
A 5.0 scale that awards bonus grade points for Advanced Placement (AP), International Baccalaureate (IB), or Honors courses.
